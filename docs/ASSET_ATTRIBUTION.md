# Asset attribution

The portal guide images under `portal/public/guide/` are product UI captures
prepared for the Kenning Trusted Agent project. They contain no customer data
or live environment identifiers.

The portal's visual mark and CSS are project assets. No third-party photograph,
portrait, font, customer logo, or hosted-site asset is required by the public
build. Phosphor icons and PDF.js are third-party dependencies; see
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and their upstream license
files for attribution.

Before adding an image, font, logo, PDF, or generated illustration, record its
source, license, permission, and removal plan here or in the relevant package
notice. Do not add marketing-site-only material to the public portal.
